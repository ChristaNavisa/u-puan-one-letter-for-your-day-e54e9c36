# One Letter for Your Day

One letter, A to Z, standing for the word that describes your day. This
entry writes the letter **Z** — behind it sits "zero sleep": a day that
started on no sleep at all, staring at the ceiling at 4 a.m., the alarm
going off anyway, walking through the whole day like a zombie. Vivid,
funny, and true.

The program writes exactly one character, `Z`, to `/output/entry.txt`
and nothing else.
